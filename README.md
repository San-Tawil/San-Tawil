## About me

I'm a bioinformatics student at the **Lebanese American University**, graduating in **2027**. I work where biology, statistics, and software engineering meet—primarily on high-throughput sequencing analysis and reproducible scientific workflows.

- 🧬 Exploring **RNA-seq**, **transcriptomics**, **microbiome analysis**, and **metagenomics**
- ⚙️ Building automated pipelines with **Snakemake**, **Nextflow**, and **Docker**
- 📊 Working with differential expression, enrichment analysis, diversity metrics, and ordination
- 🔬 Interested in computational drug discovery and AI-assisted biological research
- 🌍 Lebanese-Canadian, based in Beirut, Lebanon
